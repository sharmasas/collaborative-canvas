# Collaborative Canvas

A browser-based drawing tool where everyone connected sees everyone else's strokes appear in real time, on the same canvas.

**Live:** [https://duodoodle.netlify.app/](https://duodoodle.netlify.app/)

Collaborative Canvas is an interactive, multi-person web application for creating art. Fun and relaxing to use,  it hosts several “canvases” that produce unexpected outputs which promote creativity and a sense of calm.

The interface allows users to swtich between canvas types, refresh to start a new canvas, and save a copy of their collaborative artworks -- 

**Canvas 01_Calligraphy**
Calligraphy seeks to mimic the stroke of a brush just as if you were painting in physical space. Use this canvas to send handwritten messages, produce free hand drawings, or simply play off each other’s compositions. Different colors represent who is drawing on the canvas, and who is receiving the drawing.

**Canvas 02_Kaleidoscope**
Create complex and colorful drawings easily with the simple drag of your mouse with Kaleidoscope. Added symmetry gives the drawing a dynamic feel as well as an element of surprise. Colors change based on where your mouse is located on the screen as well as who is sending and receiving the drawing.

**Canvas 03_PixelFace**
Pixel Reveal utilizes the participants’ video cameras to bridge the gap of physical space with the digital canvas. With each click of the mouse, your canvas will begin to reveal what your computer camera sees. In return, the pixels of the other user will be revealed as they press different areas of their canvas. This creates a unique hybrid composition consisting of your faces and the different physical environments you are in.
